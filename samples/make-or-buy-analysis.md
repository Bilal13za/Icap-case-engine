# Sample 2 — Make-or-buy (Pak Components Ltd, fictional)

## Facts
- An external supplier quotes **Rs 18/unit** for component C-12 (100,000 units p.a.).
- In-house: variable cost Rs 12/unit; specific fixed cost Rs 500,000 p.a.;
  released capacity could earn Rs 200,000 elsewhere (opportunity cost).
- General fixed overhead is unavoidable and excluded (sunk for this decision).

## Issues recognised
**RULE-009 — Make-or-buy signal: supplier quote below in-house cost**
- Outsourcing may save money, but only relevant (avoidable) costs count. Allocated fixed overhead that will not disappear must be ignored, and the value of released capacity must be included.
- *Alternative explanations considered:* The in-house cost includes allocated overhead that would not actually be saved.; The quote is a loss-leader or excludes hidden costs (transport, quality control).; Quality, lead time or reliability differences outweigh the price gap.; In-house production protects a strategic capability.
- *Caution:* Indicator only — not a proven cause. Rule out alternative explanations before concluding.

## Frameworks
- **FW-MAKEBUY** (Make-or-buy analysis) — Compares in-house production cost against the supplier price, including released capacity and strategic factors.
  - *Not suitable when:* In-house production is not a realistic alternative.
  - *Why it fits this rule:* The dedicated make-vs-buy comparison.
- **FW-RELCOST** (Relevant costing) — Strips out sunk and unavoidable costs so only incremental costs drive the comparison.
  - *Not suitable when:* Cost behaviour (fixed/variable, avoidable/unavoidable) is unknown.
  - *Why it fits this rule:* Ensures only relevant costs enter the comparison.
- **FW-CAPACITY** (Capacity analysis) — Checks whether released capacity has a profitable alternative use.
  - *Not suitable when:* Capacity is not a constraint in either option.

## Calculations (full workings in the CSV/Excel files)
### Make-or-buy comparison
- Make: 100,000.00 × 12.00 + 500,000.00 (specific fixed) + 200,000.00 (opportunity) = 1,900,000.00
- Buy: 100,000.00 × 18.00 = 1,800,000.00
- Saving from making = 1,800,000.00 − 1,900,000.00 = -100,000.00
