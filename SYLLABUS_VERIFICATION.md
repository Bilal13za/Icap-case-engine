## 3. Fully verified detailed syllabuses (official)

**Directive 1.01 (Revised 2024), The Institute of Chartered Accountants of Pakistan**
(official PDF, 90 pages, downloaded from icap.org.pk 2026-10-21; extracts stored in `syllabus/`).

### 3a. CAF-6 Managerial and Financial Analysis (Scheme 2021) — predecessor of CAF-7 BIA
- Competency: "Apply business and financial analysis on business problems."
- Grid: A Managerial Analysis (weightage 30–40), B Financial Analysis and Risk Management (30–40),
  C Budgeting (25–35). Teaching hours 110–130.
- Key examinable technical competencies (proficiency/testing levels as published):
  - A-a External Analysis: political/legal/social impacts (P2/T2); technological disruption (P2/T2);
    economic environment (P2/T2); competitive environment (P2/T2).
  - A-b Internal Analysis: internal environment (P2/T2); adequacy of manual/technology-based
    systems and controls for data (P2/T2); applying ICT to enhance systems (P2/T2).
  - A-c Ethical Decision making: ethical decision-making models (P2/T2).
  - B-1 Sources of finance incl. debt, equity, Islamic finance (P1/T1).
  - B-2 Cost of equity, cost of debt, WACC (P2/T1).
  - B-3 CAPM: sensitivity to systematic risk, expected market return, risk-free assets (P2/T2).
  - B-4 Risk management techniques (P2/T2); B-5 Financial risk management techniques (P2/T2).
  - C-1 Budget preparation using external/internal analysis and quantitative techniques (P2/T2).
  - C-2 Cash flow and working capital analysis (P2/T2).
  - C-3 NPV, IRR and Payback for a given project (P2/T2).
- Specific Examinable Knowledge Reference (verbatim): SWOT Analysis; Porter's Five Forces Model;
  Product Life Cycle Model; Boston Consulting Group Matrix; Tucker's 5-question ethical model;
  American Accounting Association 7-step ethical model; ISO 31000 Risk management; CAPM;
  interest-rate hedging (futures, options, forwards); FX hedging (currency futures, options,
  forwards, money-market hedge); commodity/security forwards and futures.

### 3b. CFAP-4 Business Finance Decisions (Scheme 2021)
- Competency: "Apply technical knowledge and skills in strategic financial decision making,
  and formulating and implementing organizational financial strategies."
- Grid: 1 Business Appraisals and Securities (40–60), 2 Capital and its Sources (25–30),
  3 Financial Risk Management (20–25). Teaching hours 110–130.
- Learning outcomes (proficiency/testing levels as published):
  - A-1 business valuation appraisals (P3/T3); A-2 acquisitions/mergers/demergers/spin-offs (P3/T3);
    A-3 new projects and strategies appraisals (P3/T3); A-4 shareholder value: dividend and
    investment decisions (P2/T2); A-5 debt valuation techniques (P3/T2).
  - B-1 advising on raising capital from various sources, qualitative + quantitative (P3/T3);
    B-2 capital budgeting and rationing for investment decisions (P2/T2);
    B-3 optimal portfolio selection appraisals (P3/T2).
  - C-1 assess and manage price, currency, interest, liquidity, credit risks (P3/T3);
    C-2 evaluate implications of those risks on business (P3/T3).
- Specific Examinable Knowledge Reference (verbatim): NPV, APV, IRR, MIRR, EIRR, payback,
  capital rationing, profitability index, scenario planning, replacement and sensitivity analysis;
  valuation (dividend yield, P/E, DCF, asset-based); APT; WACC incl. dividend discount model,
  CAPM, free-cash-flow model; cost/market value of debt (effective interest, spot, YTM/YTC);
  shareholder value (dividend payout, dividend irrelevance, rights issues incl. TERP, MM theory);
  capital structure theories (traditional, Modigliani-Miller); portfolio selection (diversifiable/
  non-diversifiable risk, correlation, covariance, multi-asset portfolios); CAPM (ungearing/
  re-gearing beta, SML, over/undervalued securities); interest-rate hedging (futures, options,
  swaps, swaptions, forwards); FX hedging (futures, swaps, options, forwards, swaptions,
  money-market hedge); forwards/futures on commodities and securities; liquidity risk
  (indicators, stress testing, forecasting, contingency planning); credit risk (financial and
  non-financial analysis, factoring, insurance).

## 5. Labelling rules for the build (mandatory)

Every syllabus/LO/topic/framework row in the database MUST carry:
- `verification_status`: one of `verified_official` | `provisional_predecessor` |
  `supplementary` | `unverified`
- `official_source`: e.g. "ICAP Directive 1.01 (Revised 2024), Education and Training Scheme 2021"
- `effective_date`: e.g. "2024-11-07" (directive creation date) for 2021 content.

Rules:
- 2021 MFA/BFD rows → `verified_official`.
- 2025 BIA/SBF rows carried from predecessors → `provisional_predecessor`, with UI note
  "Detailed 2025 syllabus pending official publication; mapped from verified 2021 predecessor."
- CAPM/budgeting removal claim → `unverified`; keep the content, flag the claim.
- Frameworks not in the knowledge references (e.g. value chain, Ansoff) → `supplementary`,
  clearly labelled "supplementary business framework — examinability not verified".
- Practice questions: ALL are original practice questions (`source_verified=false`); model answers
  are "answer outlines"; rubrics are "practice rubrics — not official ICAP marking schemes".
- Do NOT invent learning outcomes, weightages, or syllabus references for 2025.
